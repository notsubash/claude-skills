# Templates

## Problem statement

[Segment] struggles to [job] because [cause], which costs them [consequence].
Evidence: [interviews, tickets, usage data]. Today they work around it by [workaround].

## Press release and FAQ (lightweight PR/FAQ)

- **Headline:** what the customer gets, in their words.
- **Two or three short paragraphs:** the problem, the new experience, why it matters, and how it works. No invented quotes.
- **FAQ:** the hard questions: pricing, data, limits, what we are not doing, what happens when it fails.

If the draft isn't compelling in the customer's language, say so: the thinking isn't ready.

## Spec outline (one to three pages)

1. Problem and evidence
2. Goals (measurable) and non-goals
3. Users and key scenarios
4. Proposed approach, with alternatives considered (including do nothing)
5. Appetite, rabbit holes, no-gos
6. Success metrics and guardrails
7. Risks, open questions, and what we'll test first
8. Rollout and rollback plan

## Experiment card

- **Hypothesis:** We believe [change] will cause [outcome] for [segment].
- **Primary metric and target:** [metric] moves [amount] within [time].
- **Guardrails:** [metrics that must not worsen].
- **Rollout:** [stages and dates].
- **Decision rule:** if [result], we [ship / iterate / kill]. Written before launch.
