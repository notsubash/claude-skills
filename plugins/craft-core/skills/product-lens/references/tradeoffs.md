# Engineering-product tradeoffs, prioritization, communication, teaching

## Tradeoffs

- **Tech debt:** express as cost to delivery or reliability (extra days per feature, incident rate), not "messy code".
- **Performance:** tie targets to user behavior in the specific flows where it matters.
- **Estimates:** ranges with stated assumptions. Estimate the appetite too.
- **Build vs buy vs reuse:** total cost of ownership, including maintenance and lock-in.
- **Reliability:** user impact and an error budget.
- **Options, not ultimatums:** "A ships in a week with limitation X; B takes six weeks and removes it. I recommend A because..."

## Prioritization

Use RICE or similar scoring to surface assumptions, not to settle debates. Weigh cost of delay: some work loses value fast (regulatory deadline, competitor launch), some doesn't. Equal scores can hide very different costs of waiting.

## Communicating a decision

State the decision needed. Give two or three options with tradeoffs. Recommend one and say why. Say what you need from the reader and by when. Leaders want impact and risk; peers want mechanism.

## Teaching mode

When the user is learning the method: explain the principle behind each step, use their real work as the example, and give one small exercise at a time (rewrite one of their tickets as a problem statement; draft three past-behavior interview questions). Check the answer and give specific feedback before the next exercise.
